* added: `FieldModes` runtime post-processing, which accumulates in every cell the Fourier coefficients in time of a scalar or vector field at multiples of a base frequency, over the last whole periods of the sampling window, and writes the mean, the real and imaginary parts and the amplitude of each mode. `mode_amplitude` and `reconstruct_modes` give the amplitude and the wave of chosen modes in every cell after the run, and `spanwise_modes` splits the coefficients into spanwise wavenumbers on a mesh extruded along the span.
* changed: a runtime post-processing object can write several fields: `build_args` of a vector of objects now collects every field of each object instead of the first.
